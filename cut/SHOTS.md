# Shot list

One shot per beat, in both formats. Beat times come from `beats` in the config and are quoted here only so the page can be read on its own. The camera code reads the config, not this file.

## Conventions

- World: meters, Y up. The vehicle rests on y = 0 at the origin and faces +Z. Its left side is +X.
- The bay grid of the deck is turned 12 degrees against the vehicle, so the vehicle sits across the lines.
- The large billboard is behind the vehicle on the -Z side, a little toward +X, so it sits right of the car in the opening frame of the master. The opening track of the master runs from 40 degrees off the nose to 26 degrees off it, so the background and the near deck move against the vehicle during the push. In the vertical format the track runs from 40 to 30 degrees off the nose and further back, so one tall tower stands over the vehicle. A wheel stop and a drain grate lie on the near deck inside the opening frame of both formats. The billboard is outside the frame to the right and reflects along the upper body surfaces when the camera looks toward the rear.
- The single red sign is on the same side as the billboard. The stair core is on the +X side near the -Z parapet.
- Focal lengths come only from `lens.focalLengthsMm`. A focal length gives the field of view across the long side of a 36 mm gate. In `formats.master` the long side is horizontal. In `formats.vertical` the camera is turned on its side, so the long side is vertical.
- The lens is anamorphic in character: oval bokeh and a restrained streak. The squeeze does not change the field of view figures above.
- Stop: T2 on the wide shots, T4 on the paint, wheel and lamp shots, T11 on the detail shot so the whole grille holds. The aperture is sampled, so depth of field follows from these.
- Camera positions name the lens entrance pupil. Heights are above the deck.
- Rig limits come from `reference/board/13-camera-moves-on-vehicles.md`: slider travel up to 1 m at up to 0.13 m per second, dolly up to 3 m of track at 0.2 to 0.6 m per second, crane and jib arm starts and stops eased over at least 1 second, pan and tilt 2 to 15 degrees per second. The `wheel` shot is on a small jib arm, not a slider, so the slider speed limit does not apply to it. The `lamp` shot is on a short dolly track, so the dolly limits apply to it.
- Easing: a smooth S curve. Each end of a move takes at least 15 percent of the move, and never less than 1 second on the dolly and the crane. The dolly and the crane keep each end to 25 percent or less. The sliders and the jib may take longer over a start that follows a cut, so the move is not at speed while the eye is still finding the frame.
- Operator motion sits on every shot: seeded, periodic over the film, at most 0.12 degrees of rotation and 2 mm of travel on the wide shots, a quarter of that on the macro shots. Frequencies 0.2 to 1.5 Hz. It never reads as shake.
- Cuts happen only at beat boundaries. `arrival` into `wake` is one continuous move with no cut. `return` begins with a cut to a raised three-quarter view and ends on the opening frame, so the loop has no cut.
- Every insert is a true macro: the lens stands 0.4 to 2.1 m from its subject on an 85 or 100 mm lens, and the subject fills the frame. No insert shows the whole wheel with body around it. Each insert has its own height, direction and move so no two read as the same setup: `paint` looks down across the wing and travels along it past the vent slot, `wheel` booms from arch to deck, `lamp` pushes from the whole signature into one projector lens, `detail` passes along the grille at a grazing angle with the edge of the panel in frame.
- Rain on the front element: none in this cut.
- The `detail` anchor is the right-hand airbox grille in the open engine bay under the rear deck: a hexagonal mesh panel 0.42 m long and 0.18 m wide with a plain margin, a flush duct behind the mesh and the stepped carbon side panel beside it. The near cam cover and the bay wall stand behind it in the grazing view.

## Checks this list has to pass

- No camera position is inside geometry or closer than 0.10 m to a surface. The nearest pass is the `wheel` jib at the end of its boom, 0.15 m above the deck at 19.97 s in both formats. The wide dolly runs 0.20 m above the deck. The `paint` slider stays 0.60 m or more from any surface, the `lamp` dolly ends 0.44 m from the lens in the master and 0.57 m in the vertical, and the `detail` slider stays 0.34 m or more from the bay.
- No frame shows the edge of the world. The parapet and the towers close every sight line used here.
- The camera values at 45.0 s equal those at 0.0 s in both formats, and the speed at both ends is zero.
- Every lens is in `lens.focalLengthsMm`.
- Slider moves measured from the camera path: master `paint` 0.19 m at 0.040 m per second peak, `detail` 0.15 m at 0.057; vertical `paint` 0.16 m at 0.034, `detail` 0.14 m at 0.053. Jib boom on `wheel`: 0.60 m of path at 0.18 m per second peak in both formats. Dolly: master push 2.4 m at 0.39 m per second peak, vertical push 1.7 m at 0.27, master `lamp` push 1.66 m at 0.50, vertical `lamp` push 0.79 m at 0.24, master `ignition` arc 0.93 m at 0.21, vertical `ignition` arc 1.0 m at 0.23. Crane: master 2.4 m at 0.34 m per second peak, vertical 1.45 m at 0.20.
- Framing numbers are targets for the blockout. Where the blockout critics ask for a change, the change is made in the camera data and this file is updated in the same commit.
