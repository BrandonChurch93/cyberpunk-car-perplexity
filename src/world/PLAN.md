# Stage B world plan

This proposes the full night world. Keep the verified gray-box and `createWorld({ detail })` intact, replace the full stand-in only after the Builder approves the next stage, and reuse the blockout's measured placement unless a shot review shows a reason to change it. The deck stays at y = 0 at the vehicle contacts, with no tall additions inside the nine-meter camera envelope.

## Construction and budgets

All geometry, masks and glyphs are made procedurally in three.js without new dependencies or background plates. Name persistent random streams by district, block, building, face, floor and bay, for example `world/city/south/03/02/west/08/04`. Give the deck, signs, services and traffic their own `world/deck`, `world/signs`, `world/services` and `world/traffic` branches. Adding a prop must not advance a stream that places a neighboring building. Keep architecturally necessary spacing regular; vary fixtures by use and age, and tie stains to bolts, joints, weather exposure and runoff rather than scattering grunge uniformly.

Use indexed geometry for bespoke silhouettes and merge compatible static surfaces by material and district. Instance repeated hardware and window pieces by block, not as one citywide mesh, so the camera, shadow, planar and cube views can cull independently. Bake deterministic instance attributes at construction. Choose fixed geometry detail by placement across all approved shots and reflection views, not by per-sample camera distance. Prefer opaque frames, room geometry and rough glass specular over citywide layered transparency.

The table gives planning allocations, not measurements. Triangles count instances at full population before culling, for one submission of the world. Texture entries count uncompressed resident mip chains, not disk size. A shared texture is charged once; geometry buffers, CPU copies, shadow targets and reflection targets are separate. Board numbers refer to the matching files in `reference/board/`. Dimensions below are prototype targets from the board; values in the film config are read through `src/config.ts`.

## Light and water handoff

### One billboard drives the light and the image

- **Source:** Use a shared set of periodic coefficients for a broad band and a few bounded spatial masks. Generate the moving RGB field on the panel in linear space. Integrate the same masks over the physical cabinet area on the CPU, including duty cycle and any out-of-service regions. Use that integral for the `FilmLight` color and level; face-on intensity starts at panel luminance times active area, in the pipeline's cd/100 units. Share one evaluator and scratch outputs across `beginFrame`, `beginSample`, `colorAt` and `level`; no pixel readback.
- **Rig:** Start with seven world lights: billboard, cool group, warm group, single red accent, stair doorway, and two deck poles. Start with shadows on the billboard and two poles. Reserve the eighth slot and fourth shadow for a measured problem, such as separating the billboard into two non-overlapping emitter regions if a single proxy cannot carry the wash across the deck. Use physical apertures, matching local +Z emitter orientation, visible occluders and plausible range. Beam flags belong on the key and appropriate poles, not every city sign. Distant emissive surfaces contribute through the environment, planar reflection and haze, not extra rig lights.
- **Timing:** `beginFrame` sets the identical world evaluator to frame-center time for the cube capture. `beginSample` sets it to `sample.timeSeconds` before the rig and planar pass. Test the field and its major motion at zero and `config.film.durationSeconds` without wrapping time. Match the first wash and return using the shot timeline, and assign flicker, train motion and beacon phase by deterministic integer cycles over film duration. Confirm the capture and haze hooks with the Builder before implementation; the current stand-in path alone cannot validate them.
- **Names:** Use `config.signage.generic` and build `signs` directly from emitted text entries, including core, barrier and payment text. Keep the hero billboard abstract and text-free, use only original Latin glyphs, numerals and simple pictograms, and avoid invented names in the first pass. Before including any `signage.invented` entry, check it for name collisions; if it needs replacing, tell the Builder to change the config and log it. No runtime network and no out-of-scope config edits.

### The deck provides relief, not a second water renderer

- **Height and masks:** `WorldModule.deck` exposes actual reflective slabs, one physical deck material, world XZ `Box2` bounds and red-channel signed height in meters. Use localized low spots and joint barriers plus millimeter relief; preserve wheel contacts and the common planar reflection plane. The large relief texture describes basin shapes and falls, not subtexel aggregate. Use high-frequency procedural roughness and normals for 5 to 20 mm aggregate, plus shared masks for markings. Check XZ orientation with the weather owner using a known drain, so the same basin appears in depth, puddle and runoff views.
- **Surface water:** Register porous concrete and deck, sheet-wet metal and glass where appropriate, and beading on coated housings and rubber. Keep corroded areas matte. Share `patchMaterial` with the surface owner rather than overwriting shader hooks. Their patch applies darkening, height-driven jagged shores, damp halos and ripple normals before the stage adds the roughness-dependent planar reflection. Compare sharp standing water with blurred film and broken reflected columns; do not add duplicate mirror planes or bake the billboards into textures.
- **Air:** Parent a `drain` steam anchor just above the selected grate's open slots and a `vent` anchor at the opening of the rooftop plant's hot flue. Follow the air owner's proposed optional `WorldModule.steamSources` entries (`object`, `kind`, optional `strength`); report both transforms and opening dimensions at integration and tune strengths in a backlit crop. Register the top-facing deck, parapet caps and relevant equipment materials in `world.surfaces` so the air system can sample splash contacts. Give the air owner candidate drip edges at rails and roof lips. Air owns splash crowns, rain layers, steam and heat shimmer.
- **Reflections and haze:** Core owns cube capture, `reflectionBox` correction, haze and beam passes. Use the actual deck plane, parapet and effective nearest surfaces for the reflection box; keep sky open. Compare neutral patches at approximately 50, 200 and 500 m with and without haze to tune the board's depth separation. Ask the Builder to verify the sky draw path and the far clip in direct and reflected views before placing any more distant towers. Stay within current clip limits unless a shot demonstrates a real need to extend them.

## Implementation order and approval checks

- **First integration:** Keep the gray-box path unchanged. Once approved, build the deck material, wetness inputs, doorway and a supported billboard with the shared emitter evaluator. Inspect both hero wides and the paint macro before adding the rest of the city. Confirm that paint, planar puddle and backlit rain change together with the lens effects disabled and that local direct light is not a substitute for environment capture.
- **Second integration:** Build the closest rooms and signs, then the middle and far streets, railway, roof clutter, aircraft and sky. Judge near window occlusion using a moving crop, not just the full wides. For repetition, crop adjacent windows, patches and signs in both directions; check the widest vertical towers and puddle before duplicating modules. Inspect the rendered seams around the entire deck and the under-deck street closure through the camera and reflection views.
- **Acceptance:** Run build, tests and deterministic fresh-load, shuffled-order and chunked still checks. Recompute clearance at all frame centers and shutter bounds with the full aperture; keep at least 0.10 m world clearance. Render every beat and the seam in preview and vertical, then inspect master and portrait hero and macro crops with `beauty`, `wetness`, `normals` and relevant reflection diagnostics. Compare representative stills to `reference/keyframes/` as framing and mood targets, never as assets. Check night separation, the red accent, paint wash, pavement shoreline, ignition puddle and clean return at phone size and in native crops.
- **Performance:** Count triangle instances and actual allocations, record calls and triangles for main, shadow, cube and planar views, and time fixed cost and per-sample cost on the same SwiftShader master frames against the pre-world pipeline. Whole-scene goals are the limits in `src/PIPELINE.md`. If cost grows too far, first reduce invisible shadow casters, transparent overdraw and unresolvable fixtures, then batch by block. Preserve the camera-facing room depth and set details that survive the approved shots.
