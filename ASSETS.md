## What is not third-party

- The vehicle. No mesh with a clean license exists for it, so it is built in code from the design grant drawings and photographs listed in `reference/vehicle/`.
- Signs, billboards, towers, rain, haze and every shader. All are made in this repo.
- Key frames in `reference/keyframes/`. They are generated look targets and never appear in the film.
