# Probe · the approved image under the photo test

Not a round and not a score for the build. On 2026-11-21, after round 8, `reference/approved/hero.jpg` was resized to 1400 px wide and shown to three fresh jurors, one per family, with the C1 juror text and nothing else. The purpose: to learn how much of the round 8 hero result (median 2) comes from what the frame shows rather than from how it is rendered.

| Reviewer | Family | Likelihood | What decided it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| reviewer_2848 | Claude | 3 | The signs are too generic and convenient: single words like HOTEL, BAR, PARKING, 24H, OPEN and EXIT placed neatly around a cyberpunk skyline; the car looks like a Lamborghini but is not a real model, with odd panel lines and asymmetric lamp shapes |
| reviewer_2849 | GPT | 2 | The vertical sign reads PARKNG, a generated-text error |
| reviewer_2850 | Gemini | 1 | Nonsensical, generic giant neon signs on office towers (BAR, 24H, OPEN, HOTEL, the garbled PARKNG and pseudo glyphs), oversized placeholder words rather than real signage |

Median 2. The round 8 render of the same frame also had a median of 2 (3, 2, 1).

What follows from it:

- On the hero frame, the jury reads the content first: single generic words at multi-storey size on towers, and a car that is no real model. A perfect render of exactly that content would still fail the floor of 50.
- Addendum 002 leaves sign lettering and vehicle identity open, so both can move toward what a photograph would show without leaving the approved look: signs keep their place, color and footprint but read as businesses (names from `signage.invented`, with small secondary lines), at sizes and mountings real signs have, some of them damaged; the vehicle moves toward the proportions and details of the named car.
- The colors, the reflections, the camera and the building layout stay as approved.
