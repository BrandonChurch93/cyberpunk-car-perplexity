## What the critics said

- C8, major: the tall ignition frame is not composed for the tall format. The left border cuts through the front wheel, the wing and the mirror, and there is unused room on the right.
- C8, major: the wide wheel macro is too tight in height and heavy on the right. The hub is cut at the bottom at the start, the wheel is cut top and bottom in the middle with a quarter of the frame blank wing on the left, and the lens never looks onto the tread.
- C8, minor: the wide lamp push ends with the second projector bezel clipped by the right border and a sliver of the running light across the lower right corner.
- C8, minor: the tall detail frame clips both side corners of the grille against the borders with dark margins above and below, travels only two or three cells, and a bright chamfer comes in at the top right at the end.
- C8, minor: the end of the wheel stop sits as a soft block in the lower right corner around 4 s, and the opening frame is heavy on the left with little room in front of the nose.

## What changes next

- Tall ignition: the whole vehicle inside the frame with a margin either side, seen from further behind so it stays large.
- Wide wheel: further back and turned toward the front of the tire, so the tread shoulder, the arch, the rim, the disc and the caliper are all in frame and the wheel is centered.
- Wide lamp: the push stops a little wider, with both projectors clear of the border and the running light either a full band or out of frame.
- Tall detail: the lens lines up with the long axis of the grille, so the grille stands in the frame as a runway with its sides clear, and the pass is longer.
- Opening: the vehicle moves toward the center so the nose has room, and the wheel stop is placed so no end of it rests in a corner.
- Before the next formal pack, draft sheets go to three development agents on three model families for comment on frame edges, tangents and balance.
