## Independent four-ray measurements

Each p95 is in inverse square metres, from four deterministic rays per native pixel (a 2 × 2 grid at 1920 × 1080). For visible painted runs, the audit samples second differences of the unit normal at 10 mm intervals in both screen directions, excluding stations near bends under a 40 mm radius. The maps show the larger valid directional value: a red line on a deliberate crease or opening edge does not prove ripple, and blue means no valid station, not a clean surface. The masks are geometric bins tied to the hub positions, not CAD surface IDs. The `paint` mesh supplies the material identity, so mirror housings using that material also enter nearby bins; the visible bin cannot be equated with a single part. Gray covers unscored surfaces.

## Triangle count and scale

The full-detail instantiated vehicle contains **1,488,129 triangles** at all three audit poses, of which **482,198** belong to `paint`. This is the active geometry draw range, indexed or non-indexed, multiplied by instance count and with no camera or occlusion culling; all 166 mesh objects have visible ancestry. It does not count the studio, lighting or particles. The same count on Step 0 is 664,778 total and 234,200 paint, so Pass 1 adds 823,351 triangles overall (2.24× total). More triangles did not make the front fields fairer.

The `paint` extent measures 4.947 m long and 2.033 m wide, with its highest point at 1.160 m above the deck; configured wheelbase is 2.779 m. The calibrated shell fit is applied to the sprung parts; the mirror housings are not part of the paint extent.
