## What the critics said

- C4, severe (8): a stray triangle across the headlamp cavity and a sawtooth brow; mesh tears at the rear hood corners; the rear underbody is a hollow shell with no wheelhouse liners; glass, roof, pillars, cowl, hood, fenders and bumper are one connected mesh with no pane, seal, cowl, wipers or shutlines; seams are grooves, not gaps between panels; the mirror stalk is a bare tube through the door skin; the tires clip flat into the deck; raw triangulation creases in the diffuser, under the taillights and around the front running light.
- C4, major: faceted silhouettes on the haunch, the roof line, the macro panel and the arch lips; exhaust tips are thin open cylinders in flat pockets; wheel stop and grates are miniature and the grates sit proud of the deck.
- C2, severe: the macro frame shows no rivulet. Major: fog strip under the fascia not tied to the projectors; rain streaks equally legible at every depth; the deck reads as one wrinkled water sheet; too few impact packets and splash glints; raised grates; large beads are neat capsules without pinned lobes or refracted color; the billboard reflection is a smeared rectangle with no body curvature.
- C3, major: macro paint reads as a flat mirror; beads are hollow lenses or flat dots; hero beads read as a noise texture; a dark net of lines in the magenta reflection on the front fender; projectors throw no beam or pool; the windscreen is nearly opaque.
- C9, severe: milky even haze that lifts the blacks; a flat pink sky where the approved image has dark storm cover with magenta-lit cloud; sparse lit windows. Major: broad soft smears on the deck in place of sharp per-sign streaks; lane line and grate layout; four parapet lamps and a thin rail; extra amber bars and cyan strips; PARKING and HOTEL sizes; framing 20 to 30 px high.
- C8, minor (4): the billboard competes with the car (declined, see below); the tall hero car feels undersized; the macro rectangle reads stronger than the panel; the tall macro has no anchoring bead or rivulet.
- C1 jurors on the renders: generic 3D city with placeholder signs, blocky buildings and grid windows; uniform ripple on the deck; a concept car with clean reflection bands; uniform 2D rain streaks; noise-free surfaces; a dotted line at the tire contact; a pasted-on grate; macro droplets evenly scattered with identical highlights, overlapping without merging, single dark pixels and speckle in the bokeh discs.

## What changes next

- Vehicle: the body is cut into separate shells with return flanges and open gaps; a separate windscreen pane with seal, cowl and wipers; headlamp opening and running light rebuilt as separate parts; closed rear underbody with liners, a framed grille and a diffuser with thickness; exhaust tips with wall thickness; a mirror with base and gasket; tires tangent to the deck with a contact patch; more segments on every silhouette curve.
- City and sky: a dark cloud layer lit magenta over the center towers; denser, varied lit windows and tower silhouettes; extra amber bars and cyan strips removed; signs rebuilt as real objects at the approved sizes; billboard set into a lit facade with a pixel grid; two parapet lamps and a post-and-rail barrier; wheel stops and grates at real size, flush; one grate; a bright dashed lane line.
- Deck: thin film against pools with shorelines, steep ring crests that carry the billboard into the near centre, impact packets, crowns and low spray, a contact shadow under the nose.
- Water on the car: beads with an inverted image, glints, a contact rim and merged irregular drops; a visible rivulet at 360; no beads in panel gaps.
- Paint and the macro camera: base coat with flake under a clear coat with orange peel; the macro restaged at a grazing angle across a crease so the billboard reflection bends and breaks.
- Light and air: each projector throws a visible cone and a pool on the deck; the fog strip under the fascia goes; rain in three depth tiers; haze that grows with distance.
- Grade: a print toe that brings the blacks down to the approved image (p1 23 to about 15) with the midtones held by exposure.
