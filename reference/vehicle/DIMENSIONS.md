## Wheels, tires and brakes

Rim dimensions are diameter by bead-seat width, not outside-flange dimensions. The tread-diameter column is a nominal unloaded calculation: rim diameter in inches times 25.4, plus twice the nominal section width times aspect ratio. The actual rolling diameter under load is not in the factory figures and must not be treated as identical to this number ([factory brochure, p. 32](https://www.lamborghini.com/original/DAM/lamborghini/facelift_2019/model_detail/revuelto/2024/brochure/06_24/REVUELTO_DIGITAL_BROCHURE_EN_2024.pdf#page=32)).

## Pixel tracing

The primary images are the manufacturer-submitted [USPTO design-grant sheets](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/D1119686). Raw sheet rasters are `ortho-001.png` through `ortho-008.png`; the upright, unscaled image-only crops are `view-003.png` through `view-008.png` under the cache folder in [VIEWS.md](VIEWS.md). Figures 3 and 4 are oriented nose left. In the table, \(s\) is the fraction of length aft of the nose and \(h\) is the fraction of height above the ground. In the Three.js module, Z in meters is \(4.947(0.5-s)\) and Y in meters is \(1.160h\), using the [factory size](https://www.lamborghini.com/original/DAM/lamborghini/facelift_2019/model_detail/revuelto/2024/brochure/06_24/REVUELTO_DIGITAL_BROCHURE_EN_2024.pdf#page=32).

- **Side scale:** Fig. 4 crop spans pixels x = 0 to 2746 and y = 0 to 654. Read the points against the 100 px grid in `grid-004.png`. Divide x by 2746 and use (h=(654-y)/654). The hub centers are near x = 661 and 2201; their span is 0.561 of the drawing length versus 0.562 in the brochure. Allocate the 2,168 mm factory remainder in the ratio 661:545 and use the factory wheelbase exactly. This puts the front hubs near Z = +1.286 m and rear hubs near Z = -1.494 m ([USPTO fig. 4](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/D1119686#page=5), [factory dimensions](https://www.lamborghini.com/original/DAM/lamborghini/facelift_2019/model_detail/revuelto/2024/brochure/06_24/REVUELTO_DIGITAL_BROCHURE_EN_2024.pdf#page=32)).
- **Plan scale:** Fig. 3 spans x = 0 to 2746 and has centerline near y = 631 px. Divide x by 2746; subtract 631 from y and divide by 1261 for an across-mirror width fraction; multiply by 2,266 mm for a lateral estimate. Do not infer height from the plan darkness or a raised wing angle ([USPTO fig. 3](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/D1119686#page=4), [factory width](https://www.lamborghini.com/original/DAM/lamborghini/facelift_2019/model_detail/revuelto/2024/brochure/06_24/REVUELTO_DIGITAL_BROCHURE_EN_2024.pdf#page=32)).
- **Elevation scale:** Figs. 6 and 7 span y = 0 to 738 and roughly x = 0 to 1424 including mirrors. Elevation height is (h=(738-y)/738); lateral offset is mirror width times ((x-712)/1424). Compare cross-section landmarks only at the depth they belong to; for instance the rear view's highest silhouette is the roof behind the wing ([USPTO figs. 6 and 7](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/D1119686#page=7)).

## Drag and cooling

The factory lists three active wing settings. Closed is the low-drag setting. High downforce is the other named extreme. The launch release says the driver can override automatic adjustment from a control on the steering wheel; the third setting is not described there ([factory launch release](https://www.audi.com/en/media-releases/lamborghini-revuelto-the-first-super-sports-v12-hybrid-hpev-15298/download)).

Relative to the outgoing flagship in high-load conditions, the manufacturer reports 61 percent higher aero efficiency and 66 percent more downforce. At maximum load it reports 33 percent more front load and 74 percent more rear load. Neither statement supplies an absolute drag coefficient or measured frontal area. Do not use width × height as frontal area ([factory launch release](https://www.audi.com/en/media-releases/lamborghini-revuelto-the-first-super-sports-v12-hybrid-hpev-15298/download)).

Front brake cooling uses wheelhouse airflow and the rear takes its feed from the underbody. The central front floor feeds four curved vortex generators that direct flow toward the diffuser. The nose splitter, side blade feeding the side intake, roof recess and underfloor are working surfaces; keep their shapes continuous with the visible outlets ([factory brochure, p. 19](https://www.lamborghini.com/original/DAM/lamborghini/facelift_2019/model_detail/revuelto/2024/brochure/06_24/REVUELTO_DIGITAL_BROCHURE_EN_2024.pdf#page=19), [factory launch release](https://www.audi.com/en/media-releases/lamborghini-revuelto-the-first-super-sports-v12-hybrid-hpev-15298/download)).
