# Revuelto lamps and startup

What the real car does from unlock to a running V12, with sources. Timestamps are given as minutes:seconds into the video.

## Read this first

- Video frames could not be downloaded in this task (the video site asked to sign in for every attempt). Everything from video below comes from the spoken transcript, chapter list or description of that video. So it is what the presenter says happens, not something I timed from frames.
- Because of that, there are no frame timed lamp timings, cranking times or shake measurements here. Where a value is missing it says so, and any suggested value for the kit is marked as a proposal.
- Starting the car and firing the V12 are separate events on this car. That matches the kit's `wake` beat (lamps) and `ignition` beat (engine) well.

## 1. Unlock

What happens, in order:

1. Key fob unlock. [Faisal Khan review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lo2kpeG_bgY) at about 3:10 shows the key: "Unlock the car, lock the car, and this is to open the frunk". The fob looks like an ordinary group key.
2. No welcome light animation. Faisal Khan at about 2:58: "There's no welcome animation in this car, which is a bit disappointing because Lamborghini is owned by Audi and Audi obviously does a lot of play with lights." And at about 6:44: "Again, no dynamic function here. Does not even have the welcome or goodbye animation."
3. Light settings exist for "coming home leaving home" in the settings menu ([DragTimes how to drive](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ePWtWM36UFU) at about 7:17). On most cars of this group these switch lamps on as a static courtesy light; no source says how they look on this car.
4. Door: [MilesPerHr](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RMaG8XYXWKc) at about 1:29 to 1:42 says you press the pad on the door handle with the key nearby, wait about half a second, and the door drops about half an inch before you lift it. The scissor door then rises up and forward ([DragTimes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ePWtWM36UFU) at about 1:28 to 1:40).

Not found in any source: whether the indicators flash on unlock, and how many times.

## 2. Front lamps

- DRL: the Y light guides (see PARTS.md section 3). Faisal Khan at about 2:40 to 2:58 says the DRL is very bright at night, that it turns into the indicator, and that the unit holds low beam and high beam. Photos of the lit DRL: [Opera Unica 001](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lamborghini_Revuelto_Opera_Unica_001.jpg) (dark show), [Paris 2026 white car](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lamborghini_Revuelto_(2026)_(55082446860).jpg). Both show the guides lit all at once in uniform cool white, with no visible segments or running pattern.
- Low beams lit with DRL: [Adelaide Rally 028A4530](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lamborghini_Revuelto_at_the_2025_Shannons_Adelaide_Rally_(028A4530).jpg) on a wet street, two small bright points inside each eye slot.
- [TheCarKraze walkaround](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yEkzaKGqUR8) at about 1:16: the DRL is three separate pieces with small Y and hexagon patterns inside.
- Hazards: [Quwayne reviews](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nh_oTnswI) at about 6:25 to 6:48 shows the emergency lights front and rear ("that's what it looks like from the front ... take a look at the rear"). The transcript does not say whether they sweep or flash all at once.

## 3. Tail lamps and rear

- The tail lamps are thin sideways Y light guides with a white-yellow core and red halo when lit ([Brilliant Black 02](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lamborghini_Revuelto_Brilliant_Black_02.jpg), [Opera Unica 002 at night](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lamborghini_Revuelto_Opera_Unica_002.jpg)).
- TheCarKraze at about 4:25: inside the lamps are hexagons built from small Y patterns, and the high brake lamp lights up the engine bay when you brake. In Brilliant Black 02 the third brake lamp across the top rear of the engine bay is lit along with the tails.
- Quwayne reviews has a chapter "02:20 TAILLIGHTS"; the transcript gives no behaviour detail.
- No source describes a tail lamp animation on lock, unlock or start.

## 4. Start: car on, silent

1. Foot on brake, flip up the red cover on the centre tunnel, press the button. [Road and Track](https://www.roadandtrack.com/reviews/a61697632/2024-lamborghini-revuelto-review/): "flip up dramatic red cover, depress start button with prejudice ... and something unusual happens: nothing." Faisal Khan at about 16:12 shows lifting the cover and pressing start. MilesPerHr at about 2:16 to 2:53 calls it the missile style cover.
2. The car comes on in Città (city) mode, electric only, silent. Road and Track: "The Revuelto starts up in Città (City) mode, propelled by electrons for silent running." Same in [Car and Driver](https://www.caranddriver.com/reviews/a63738645/2024-lamborghini-revuelto-test/), [Mr. Automotive](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=INENZYP_SH8) at about 2:01 to 2:09 ("will be always starting in electric drive mode"), [TopherDrives](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DsD_aapulT8) at about 3:50 to 4:03, and [Will Motivation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPz2uDvxVow) at about 15:36 ("It starts up in electric mode"). Faisal Khan at about 3:16 notes the car is on but silent.
3. Screens wake with an animation. Faisal Khan at about 18:23: "the screen shows Revolto with a nice animation when you turn on the car first thing. And then obviously it has a goodbye animation in both the screens." TheCarKraze at about 8:05 also shows the three screens and the startup animation. For a debadged car, use a plain graphic.
4. Some owners find the button logic fiddly. [DragTimes long term review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1zEychnQU9c) at about 4:52 to 8:28 says one press turns the car on, and the V12 starts only when you turn the mode dial to Strada, with presses sometimes turning the car off instead.

## 5. V12 fires

How it is triggered:

- Turn the left mode dial on the wheel from Città to Strada, Sport or Corsa. Road and Track: "Tab the red dial on the steering wheel over to Strada (Street) Sport (duh) or Corsa (Race), and it makes sense: the V-12 cold-starts with a roar on the roll." DragTimes how to drive at about 11:34 to 11:48: "spin it over to strata and there you go the engine comes to life and roars up". Faisal Khan at about 16:44 to 16:56: "As soon as I turn this, see how the engine fires up ... All of a sudden, the engine fires up with so much enthusiasm."
- Or automatically. [doubleclutch](https://doubleclutch.ca/2025/09/04/2025-lamborghini-revuelto-review) says the car does not fire the V12 until you choose a mode "or until it has nearly depleted its 3.8 kW battery." Car and Driver: "The engine remains asleep until the transmission warms up, even if you select Strada, Sport, or Corsa mode." [Edmunds](https://www.edmunds.com/car-news/2025-lamborghini-revuelto-first-drive-review.html): "You don't get much time to slip away before the giant V12 fires up."

What it sounds and looks like:

- Cranking is not heard. The starter is the rear e-motor, behind the V12 ([brochure](https://www.lamborghini.com/original/DAM/lamborghini/facelift_2019/model_detail/revuelto/2024/brochure/REVUELTO_DIGITAL_BROCHURE_EN_2024.pdf): "The rear e-motor is mounted right behind the V12 engine and functions as a starter for the engine itself"; Faisal Khan at about 22:36 says the same). doubleclutch: "No warning, no starter whirring, just an abrupt crack of thunder." So model the fire as near instant. No source gives a cranking time in seconds. Proposal: under 0.5 s from trigger to first fire. That is my estimate, not measured.
- Loud, louder when cold. [Better Half Drive](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laZCR5icAVg) at about 1:37 to 1:44: "It shocked me when that V12 fired up for the first time, especially the cold start cuz when it's cold, it's even louder." Edmunds: "when it starts, you and everyone within 100 yards will know." Car and Driver: "it fires up dramatically before settling into a warm idle."
- Cold, the engine holds a high idle and will not rev. Better Half Drive at about 2:38 to 2:53: "when it fires up, you're limited to idle. You're at 1500 RPM. It's a high idle, but it's getting everything to temperature." [JB-Motors](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K101mx9HMpQ) at about 16:25 also says it stops you revving when cold. Faisal Khan at about 16:54: the screen says "only E mode is possible while engine warming up", so the electric motors drive the car while the V12 idles.
- Warm idle: about 1000 rpm. doubleclutch: "It needs to idle at a grand just to stay alive." and "The engine idles smoothly".
- Progressive redline while warming: about 6000 rpm cold creeping to 9500 ([Motor1](https://www.motor1.com/features/743921/lamborghini-revuelto-hybrid-powertrain-review/)); about 6500 rising toward 9500 ([Alanis King](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GZHcQcF7WvY) at about 10:54 to 11:54); from about 5000 ([MilesPerHr](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RMaG8XYXWKc) at about 6:24 to 6:41); shown as 7000 limit when not warm ([DragTimes long term](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1zEychnQU9c) at about 10:35). Use this for a tachometer graphic if the kit has one.
- In town the exhaust gets quiet; DragTimes long term at about 2:56 to 3:07 says the exhaust valves do not open until about 3500 to 4000 rpm.
- Hot air at the outlets even at idle: Road and Track says the pipes "spew hot air like a hair dryer even at idle". This is a source for heat shimmer at the two outlets once the V12 runs.

Very cold starts:

- [Ice drifting video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gfNbZ3zLLj4) at about 0:49 to 1:54, at minus 2 degrees C on the dash, the car "almost sound like you didn't want to start ... I heard some clicking". At about 4:16: "Just idle. This thing is howling."
- TopherDrives at about 4:05 to 4:30, a cold morning: "the V12 started and then it like misfired and stalled", then later fired normally.
- Cold start clips with chapters but no description of the look: [Quwayne reviews](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nh_oTnswI) chapters "00:18 COLD START" and "09:21 COLD START + REVVS"; [Raw Car Sounds straight piped car](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kBbDSugoXv8) "0:00 Intro, cold start up" (aftermarket exhaust, do not use for the stock sound).

## 6. Shut off

- Turning the mode back to Città or pressing the EV button stops the V12 while the car stays on. DragTimes how to drive at about 14:43 to 14:57: "press this EV button ... and there we go the motor just shut off". MilesPerHr says switching to Città shuts off the V12.
- The screens play a goodbye animation (Faisal Khan at about 18:23). No goodbye light show (Faisal Khan at about 6:44).

## 7. Not found

I found no source, in text or transcript, for:

- Body or exhaust tip shake at start or idle.
- Steam or vapour at the outlets in cold wet weather. The Adelaide Rally photos are wet but show the front only.
- A timed cranking duration.
- Indicator flashes on unlock or lock.
- Any lamp sweep or segment order.

Anything the kit uses for these is an invention and should be labeled as one.
