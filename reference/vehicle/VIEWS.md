# Vehicle reference views

Trace from the body design grant, not from perspective photography or the dimensions printed on third-party wrap drawings. The studio set is useful for checking surfaces, seams and finishes against the patent. Cache paths below are relative to `reference/cache/vehicle/views/`; source images are visual reference only, not production assets.

## Factory drawings filed with the USPTO

[US D1,119,686 S in the USPTO Official Gazette](https://patentsgazette.uspto.gov/week12/OG/html/1544-4/USD1119686-20260324.html) identifies designer Mitja Borkert, application 29/860,622 filed November 21, 2022, and EU priority **009039043-0001**, filed May 23, 2022. The drawing matches the base production body, with the recessed exposed engine, Y-shaped lights, two high central outlets and the stowed wing. The [USPTO grant PDF](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/D1119686) was downloaded as `D1119686.pdf`; each image below is the native sheet raster extracted without resampling. The registry link opens the image on its PDF page. The [Gazette's standalone image](https://patentsgazette.uspto.gov/week12/OG/html/1544-4/USD1119686-20260324-D00000.gif) is also cached as `car-gazette.gif`, 564 × 400 px.

Related US design grants cover the [steering wheel, D1,040,712 S](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/D1040712), [dashboard, D1,104,892 S](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/D1104892), and [door panel, D1,104,893 S](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/D1104893). The documents are cached under their grant numbers as PDFs. The two division records list EU design priorities in the 009041155 series ([dashboard Gazette](https://patentsgazette.uspto.gov/week49/OG/html/1541-2/USD1104892-20251209.html), [door-panel Gazette](https://patentsgazette.uspto.gov/week49/OG/html/1541-2/USD1104893-20251209.html)).

For Europe, the US grant confirms priority number 009039043-0001, but no independent [EUIPO registry](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/search) drawing file was retrieved. No separate Hague registration or additional drawings were verified through the [WIPO Global Design Database](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-design-database/). The [later fixed-wing derivative filing](https://www.motor1.com/news/804770/lamborghini-revuelto-sv-patent-leak/) is not the geometry target.
