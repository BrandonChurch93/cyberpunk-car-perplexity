# Revuelto parts reference

Part by part notes for building the car at macro distance. The model is debadged: every crest, wordmark, script and "Revuelto" plate seen in the photos below is left off. Where a photo shows a badge, model the surface under it as plain.

## How to read this file

- Every photo cited here was downloaded to `reference/cache/vehicle/parts/commons/cNNN.jpg` (1920 px) and viewed, most also at full size in `big/` and as crops in `crops/`. Contact sheets are in `sheets/`. The id (c005 and so on) matches `commons_list.tsv`.
- Known dimensions come from the press release ([Lamborghini press release](https://www.audi.com/en/media-releases/lamborghini-revuelto-the-first-super-sports-v12-hybrid-hpev-15298/download)) and the [2024 brochure](https://www.lamborghini.com/original/DAM/lamborghini/facelift_2019/model_detail/revuelto/2024/brochure/REVUELTO_DIGITAL_BROCHURE_EN_2024.pdf): length 4947, width 2033 (2266 with mirrors), height 1160, wheelbase 2779, tracks 1720 front and 1701 rear. All sizes in mm.
- Sizes marked "est." are measured off photos. The scale used is given each time. Three scales are used:
  - S1, c113 near straight front, 520 mm Korean plate, about 1.58 mm per px at 1238 px width.
  - S2, c101 near straight rear, 520 mm EU plate, about 2.16 mm per px at 1312 px width.
  - S3, c050 near true side, wheelbase 2779 between hub centres, about 4.16 mm per px at 1300 px width. The car is a few degrees off square in this photo, so long lengths read about 4 percent short.
  - Patent views: for plan and rear checks the orthographic design grant sheets cached by the other researcher (`reference/cache/vehicle/views/view-007.png` rear, `view-008.png` bottom, see VIEWS.md) were looked at. Rear view scale: 2266 mirror to mirror over 1195 px of a 1200 px wide copy, about 1.90 mm per px.
- Photo estimates are good to about plus or minus 10 percent. Use DIMENSIONS.md first when it disagrees.

## 1. Overall body surfacing and character lines

Shape. A low wedge with one sharp centre line. [Autoblog](https://www.autoblog.com/reviews/i-drove-the-lamborghini-revuelto-its-part-rocket-part-sculpture-all-insanity-heres-my-review) calls the body "a landscape of creased surfaces and negative radiuses, with the iconic single center line running from nose to tail". In side view (c050, c047) the roofline is one straight shallow slope from the nose tip over the windscreen and roof to the rear deck. The lower body is cut by two main creases:

- A crease that starts at the top of the front arch, runs back along the door at about door handle height, and dives down into the front edge of the side intake. It falls front to rear by about 1 to 2 degrees in c050. The upper door surface is convex above it and concave (negative radius) below it.
- From the door's rear edge, a second crease climbs up and back over the rear haunch to the tail lamp. Together with the intake edge this makes the sideways Y that the designer describes: "Y-shape in the front, Y-shape in the rear, Y-shape in the side" ([evo design story](https://www.evo.co.uk/lamborghini/205802/lamborghini-revuelto-the-design-story)).

The surfaces are big and flat-ish, broken by sharp creases. Crease radii are small: at macro distance, model them as a 2 to 4 mm fillet between two near flat surfaces. Do not round them. In c005 and c120, reflections break sharply across the bonnet and rear deck creases, which shows how tight they are. Both front and rear end in sharp faceted surfaces. Facet edges have a small radius, and the facets meet at points.

Sizes. Overall 4947 x 2033 x 1160 ([press release](https://www.audi.com/en/media-releases/lamborghini-revuelto-the-first-super-sports-v12-hybrid-hpev-15298/download)). In S3, the belt (lower edge of the side glass) sits about 830 to 870 above the ground, and the front arch peak about 720 (est.). Use DIMENSIONS.md for the authoritative fractions.

Material and finish. Painted carbon panels. The bonnet is carbon fibre ([press release](https://www.audi.com/en/media-releases/lamborghini-revuelto-the-first-super-sports-v12-hybrid-hpev-15298/download)). Gloss solid or metallic paint (orange c005, yellow c047, magenta c113, white c100) or matte (black c016). Clear coat is deep and glassy, with crisp reflections, little orange peel visible at photo resolution.

Neighbors. Black lower body (sills, diffuser, splitter, intake frames) runs all around below the painted body. The line where paint meets black follows the creases rather than a flat horizontal line.

## 2. Front bumper, splitter and intakes

Shape. Seen straight on (c113, c055, c130), the nose is a sharp V in plan view, with the centre point the furthest forward. The front face has:

- A painted centre nose panel shaped like a downward pointing shield, wide at the top under the bonnet tip and narrowing to the splitter. In c113 it is about 620 wide at the top and 340 at the bottom (est., S1).
- Two large outer intakes, one each side, shaped like a lopsided hexagon. Each is about 425 wide and 270 tall (est., S1). Each is framed by the DRL light guides along the top and outer edge (section 3). Inside: a dark radiator core with horizontal fins (c100), and a carbon twill blade that crosses the top of the intake just under the DRL arm. A small round camera or sensor sits inboard near the bottom of the left intake opening (c100).
- A centre lower opening under the nose shield, the full width between the outer intakes, about 120 to 160 tall (est., S1), with a black mesh. The licence plate sits across it on EU and Korean cars. Leave it plain.
- A splitter lip in black or exposed carbon, sticking out about 30 to 50 (est. from c111, c100). The [press release](https://www.audi.com/en/media-releases/lamborghini-revuelto-the-first-super-sports-v12-hybrid-hpev-15298/download) describes the splitter's leading edge as having a radial shape in the centre. In c130 and c113 the splitter edge follows the V of the nose, curving back at the outer corners.
- The outer corners of the bumper end in painted vertical blades that wrap to the front arch. On the magenta car (c113 to c118) these outer blades, and the strip under the intakes, are painted body colour. On the white car at Quail (c128) a red accent stripe runs along the lower lip.

Material and finish. Painted shield and corners. Satin black plastic intake frames. Gloss black or exposed carbon blades and splitter, depending on spec (c100 shows carbon twill blade, c111 shows gloss black).

Neighbors. The bonnet's leading edge sits directly on the top of the headlamp slots and the nose shield. The shut line runs along the top of the eye (section 3). The intakes wrap round the corner into the front arch, where a small amber or smoked side marker sits on the front fender (c100).

## 3. Headlamp units (Y DRL, projector modules, lens, inside)

Shape. Two parts per side.

The eye. A narrow smoked slot tucked under the bonnet edge, wider at the outboard end, like a stretched trapezoid that tilts up toward the outside. It is about 205 wide in projection and about 45 to 60 tall (est., S1). The lens is dark smoked clear and follows the bonnet's surface. Behind the lens, in c100 and c116, you can see a dark housing with one main projector module (a round lens about 50 to 60 across, est.) and a smaller second element outboard. In c130 the low beams are lit and show as two small bright points per side inside the slot.

The Y. The DRL is a set of light guides shaped like a sideways Y. [TheCarKraze](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yEkzaKGqUR8) at 1:16 says the DRL is three separate pieces with small Y and hexagon patterns inside. The arms are:

- A long horizontal arm that runs inboard along the top edge of the outer intake, about 310 long in projection (est., S1).
- A node at the outboard end of that arm, under the outer end of the eye.
- An upper arm that runs up and outboard along the eye's lower edge to the bonnet corner.
- A lower arm that runs down and outboard along the intake's outer edge toward the front corner.

The guides are about 12 to 18 wide (est. from c125 and c055), flat faced, set in a dark bezel. Lit, they are a uniform cool white with no visible dots (c125, c055, c129, c130). Unlit, they look pale grey or frosted. The two Y nodes are about 1575 apart (est., S1).

Per the [Faisal Khan review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lo2kpeG_bgY) around 2:40 to 2:58, the DRL also acts as the turn indicator, and the unit holds low beam and high beam. The [Autoblog review](https://www.autoblog.com/reviews/i-drove-the-lamborghini-revuelto-its-part-rocket-part-sculpture-all-insanity-heres-my-review) describes the Y DRLs as "sharp, angular, and unmistakable at any distance".

Material and finish. Polycarbonate lens, smoked. Black textured inner bezel. Chrome-free.

Neighbors. The bonnet edge sits directly above the eye. In c100 and c113 the shut line runs along the top of the eye, not above it. The Y's horizontal arm forms the upper edge of the intake opening; the carbon blade sits just under it.

## 4. Bonnet

Shape. One wide carbon panel ([press release](https://www.audi.com/en/media-releases/lamborghini-revuelto-the-first-super-sports-v12-hybrid-hpev-15298/download)) from the nose point back to the base of the windscreen. It is shaped like a flattened arrowhead with:

- A sharp centre ridge that carries the body centre line, rising slightly from the nose to the screen.
- Two flat facets each side of the ridge that fall to the fenders. In c111 and c116 they read as two planes with a crisp crease between them.
- A shallow V-shaped dip just in front of the windscreen (c111, c117), where the bonnet surface flows into the cowl.

The front shut line follows the top of each headlamp eye and comes to a point at the nose. The side shut lines run back along the top of the front fenders, inboard of the fender peaks, to the A pillar base (c111, c117). There is no air outlet in the bonnet.

Material and finish. Carbon fibre, painted body colour. Gloss.

Neighbors. Headlamp eyes at the front corners, fenders at the sides, windscreen cowl at the rear. The front trunk lies under it.

Sources: [c111](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lamborghini_Revuelto_IMG01.jpg), [c116](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lamborghini_Revuelto_LB744_Viola_Bast_(3).jpg), [c117](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lamborghini_Revuelto_LB744_Viola_Bast_(4).jpg), [c113](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lamborghini_Revuelto_LB744_Viola_Bast_(1).jpg), [c100](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lamborghini_Revuelto_DSC_6985.jpg), [c130](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lamborghini_Revuelto_at_the_2025_Shannons_Adelaide_Rally_(028A4530).jpg), [press release](https://www.audi.com/en/media-releases/lamborghini-revuelto-the-first-super-sports-v12-hybrid-hpev-15298/download).

## 5. Windscreen, side glass, roof and A pillar

Shape. The windscreen is very raked and wraps strongly at the sides, so in front view its lower corners curve round into the A pillars (c113, c130). In side view (c050) the screen continues the line of the bonnet almost without a break. The A pillar is slim and painted body colour, with a gloss black edge where it meets the glass.

The side glass (c050, c047) is a long low wedge: the top edge follows the roof, the lower edge (belt) rises gently from front to rear, and the rear corner is sharp. Behind the door glass, a small fixed black panel or quarter glass tapers to the rear corner. A black trim strip frames the glass top.

The roof is narrow in plan view. The [press release](https://www.audi.com/en/media-releases/lamborghini-revuelto-the-first-super-sports-v12-hybrid-hpev-15298/download) says it is hollowed to channel air to the rear intakes, and [Autoblog](https://www.autoblog.com/reviews/i-drove-the-lamborghini-revuelto-its-part-rocket-part-sculpture-all-insanity-heres-my-review) says the roof directs airflow to the rear wing. In c005 and c120 there is a shallow channel in the roof down the centre, flanked by two raised rails that carry on as the two edges of the open engine bay. Behind the cabin, a small near vertical rear window sits between these rails (c005).

Sizes. Windscreen width at the base about 1350 to 1450 (est., S1). Side glass length about 1300 (est., S3), height at the B area about 330 (est., S3).

Material and finish. Laminated glass with a dark tint band along the top; black ceramic frit border about 20 to 40 wide around the screen (est.). Gloss paint roof; some cars have an exposed carbon roof.

Neighbors. The door top edge is cut into the roof (the door is a scissor door hinged at the A pillar base, section 6). The roof rails become the engine bay sides.

Sources: [c050](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lamborghini_Revuelto_(2024)_(53621960073).jpg), [c047](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lamborghini_Revuelto_(2024)_(53621749676).jpg), [c113](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lamborghini_Revuelto_LB744_Viola_Bast_(1).jpg), [c130](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lamborghini_Revuelto_at_the_2025_Shannons_Adelaide_Rally_(028A4530).jpg), [c005](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2023_Lamborghini_Revuelto_6.jpg), [c120](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lamborghini_Revuelto_LB744_Viola_Bast_(7).jpg), [press release](https://www.audi.com/en/media-releases/lamborghini-revuelto-the-first-super-sports-v12-hybrid-hpev-15298/download), [Autoblog](https://www.autoblog.com/reviews/i-drove-the-lamborghini-revuelto-its-part-rocket-part-sculpture-all-insanity-heres-my-review).

## 6. Doors, side blade and side intake

Shape. Scissor doors. In c111, c112, c091 and c128 they rise up and forward, pivoting near the base of the A pillar, and stand almost vertical when open. The door carries part of the roof, the full side glass and the upper side surface down to the sill. Its rear edge runs down at an angle and forms the front edge of the side intake.

The door surface (c050) has the main crease (section 1). Below it the panel is strongly concave and runs into the big side intake. A dark line (a gloss black strip about 15 to 20 wide, est., S3) runs down the door from the rear of the glass toward the intake. This, with the intake, makes the side Y the designer talks about ([evo](https://www.evo.co.uk/lamborghini/205802/lamborghini-revuelto-the-design-story)).

Side intake (c050, c049): a big black pocket shaped like a triangle pointing forward, about 490 long and 390 tall (est., S3), sitting in the rear part of the door and the front of the rear haunch. [bistrighini](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cZ4wDN-Ms4c) at about 1:04 to 1:15 describes a NACA duct here that points forward, with carbon accents, and around 3:00 says the pocket under the window is more of an air pocket than a full vent. [Autoblog](https://www.autoblog.com/reviews/i-drove-the-lamborghini-revuelto-its-part-rocket-part-sculpture-all-insanity-heres-my-review) says side intakes behind the doors feed the engine and cool the brakes. The [press release](https://www.audi.com/en/media-releases/lamborghini-revuelto-the-first-super-sports-v12-hybrid-hpev-15298/download) mentions NACA ducts ahead of the rear wheels.

Front side fin: a short vertical blade just behind the front wheel arch (c050, c100, c111), black or carbon, about 250 tall (est., S3). The press release calls these side fins behind the front arches.

Door handle: the press release mentions Y-section door handles. In c111 the opened door shows no exterior pull handle. ## 12. Two high hexagonal exhaust outlets and surround

Each outlet has:

## 20. Visible carbon parts and weave

Parts seen in exposed carbon: diffuser and fins (c005), sills (c053), intake blades (c100), engine bay covers, frame and louvers (c005, c120), splitter on some cars, interior console frame and steering wheel spokes (c092 shows a herringbone or chevron pattern on the wheel spoke).

Weave. 2 x 2 twill. In c005 at about 1.4 mm per px (scaled from the 300 outlet width), the diagonal ribs on the diffuser upper deck repeat every 3 to 4 px, which gives a tow width of about 2 to 3 (est., at the edge of what the photo resolves). The [Edmunds first drive](https://www.edmunds.com/car-news/2025-lamborghini-revuelto-first-drive-review.html) says the chassis mixes CFRP and forged composite; forged composite has a marbled, not woven, look, but I did not see it on an exterior part in the cited photos.
