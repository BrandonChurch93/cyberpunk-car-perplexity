## Rooftop car park decks

What to learn: Layout, bay markings, drains, joints, kerbs, parapets, stair cores, ramps, barriers, pay stations, plant, light poles, wear

### Real-world sizes

These are reference values from different places and products, not a single code for the fictional site.

- Bay width and length: 2.4 × 4.8 m on older off-street sites, 2.5 × 5.0 m for new spaces ([Wokingham Borough Council](https://www.wokingham.gov.uk/foi/car-parking-bay-sizes)).
- Bay line width: 4 in minimum, or 102 mm, measured across one stripe; stall widths may be measured from stripe centers ([Chicago parking area design, §17-10-1005](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/chicago/latest/chicago_il/0-0-0-2683095)).
- Drive aisle at 90° parking: 6.00 m one-way or 6.95 m two-way in the cited layout table ([SteelConstruction.info, dimensions and column spacing](https://steelconstruction.info/sectors/retail-buildings/car-parks/)).
- Ramp divider kerb: 230 mm wide and 150 mm exposed height in a two-way ramp example ([Kuala Lumpur car parking guidelines, §2.4](https://es.scribd.com/document/354269947/Guidelines-for-Car-Parking-DBKL-pdf)). An edge-restraint half-battered precast unit is 150 mm wide × 305 mm total unit height × 914 mm long; the unit height is not the exposed rise ([Bradstone specification](https://source.thenbs.com/en-us/gb/product/bradstone-british-standard-kerbs/tgwEkwTDcFvex4am1Neprk/isNSBfXev9iQ73QSBVDHs3?previousVersion=vLBKVYjNXiGNLTFp4QqYNK)).
- Concrete wheel stop: 1,650 × 150 × 100 mm, length × width × height ([Dallcon](https://dallcon.com.au/product/concrete-wheel-stop/)).
- Concrete parapet and steel rail: one roof vehicle-storage rule gives a 24 in minimum solid wall and a 42 in top height above the roof when a railing is added, about 0.610 m solid with 0.457 m above it to the top rail if the wall is at its minimum ([New York motor vehicle storage rules, §1506.1(f)](https://govt.westlaw.com/nycrr/Document/I4f70b062cd1711dda432a117e6e0f345?viewType=FullText&originationContext=documenttoc&transitionType=CategoryPageItem&contextData=(sc.Default))). The general IBC guard-height example is also 42 in from the walking surface ([IBC §1015.3](https://codes.iccsafe.org/s/IBC2021P1/chapter-10-means-of-egress/IBC2021P1-Ch10-Sec1015.3)).
- Ramp gradients: 5 to 6% for a ramp shared with parking, 10 to 15% for straight speed ramps without flanking stalls or foot traffic, 12% suggested maximum for long ramps, transitions above 14%, and a typical single lane 12 ft clear between kerbs, or 3.66 m ([AISC open-deck parking structures, Appendix B](https://www.aisc.org/globalassets/why-steel/parking-design-aid.pdf)).
- Barrier arm: 3,000 × 100 × 45 mm overall, length × width × height per supplier; hollow aluminum with a rubber lower strip ([Dahua straight parking arm](https://previous.dahuasecurity.com/products/All-Products/Intelligent-Traffic/Smart-Parking-Products/Accessories/Barrier-Arm/IPMECD-0232-M30)). Other stock arm lengths include 3.5 and 4 m for wider approaches ([Dahua barrier data sheet](https://www.dahuasecurity.com/asset/upload/uploads/soft/20210220/DHI-IPMECD-2032_datasheet_20200927.pdf)).
- Light pole mounting height and spacing: one city limits roof deck fixture bottoms to 15 ft above the deck, or 4.57 m ([West Richland §17.55.050](https://www.codepublishing.com/WA/WestRichland/html/WestRichland17/WestRichland1755.html)). The 15 to 20 ft outdoor pole class is paired with 30 to 50 ft typical spacing, or 9.1 to 15.2 m, in a manufacturer guide; this is a spacing starting point, not a roof standard ([Jarvis Lighting](https://www.jarvislighting.com/blogs/jarvis-lighting-insights/parking-lot-lighting-design-guide)).
- Drain grate assembly: round 200 mm top diameter or square 200 × 200 mm top section, both with lockable grates and nominal 100 mm outlets ([ACO car park gullies](https://www.aco.co.uk/media/v3/8/672514C4_8B7B.pdf)). The dimensions are for the full top, including its frame.
- Expansion joint width: 25 to 140 mm clear opening at mean temperature across a trafficable membrane and nosing system for exposed top decks ([Sika Emseal parking deck expansion joint](https://www.emseal.com/product/thermaflex-parking-deck-expansion-joint/)). This is not the full width of the exposed repair shoulders.

### Build notes

- Start with 2.50 × 5.00 m stalls, 0.102 m stripe width and a 6.95 m two-way aisle. Set neighboring stall centers 2.50 m apart. Leave a clear 16.95 m wall-to-wall parking module before local islands or plant. Let the car read as deliberately angled across a line, not mistaken for a narrow stall.
- Use 0.61 m of solid concrete with the rail top at about 1.07 m above the walking surface. Repeat wall piers on the structural grid, but vary chips, pour tones, algae, and drips below post feet. Keep the rail attached to real members with visible bases and anchors.
- Give the speed ramp a 3.66 m clear lane and a 10% running slope with a softened crown and toe. Keep the barrier and drive-up reader on the flatter approach. Use a 3.5 m arm on a kerbed control island, with a dark underside strip, red reflectors and an articulated hinge housing.
- Start the lamp heads at 4.55 m and space them about 12 m apart. Use plausible paired heads and opaque housings. Break up the wet deck's highlights between pools rather than painting the whole slab one gray value.
- Model a 0.20 m square point-drain top, a near-flush grate with dark open slots and a shallow dished patch draining toward it. Set the major deck joint at 0.050 m nominal width, with a separate seal and repair shoulders. Do not render a permanent open trench from the construction photo.
- At a low camera, require visible tire scuff across an aisle end, one offset concrete patch with a distinct edge, isolated vertical parapet runoff, and a bright reflection ending at a different place from its wet patch. Give utility boxes, vent feet and stair doorway thresholds small real shadows and visible support.
