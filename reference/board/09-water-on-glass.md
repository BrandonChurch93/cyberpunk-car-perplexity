## Water on glass

What to learn: Rivulet paths, film, distortion of what is behind

Cached copies: `reference/cache/board/water-on-glass/` (g01 to g14; g08 not used because its provenance was unclear).

### Sources

#### S1 · Floodlight through heavy rain on a window
- URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Four_versions_of_a_floodlight_through_rain_on_a_window.jpg
- Image: https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/24/Four_versions_of_a_floodlight_through_rain_on_a_window.jpg/1280px-Four_versions_of_a_floodlight_through_rain_on_a_window.jpg
- Shows: Four frames of one floodlight seen through heavy rain running down double glazing, on black. (cache g01)
- Observations:
  1. The flowing water film breaks the single floodlight into a web of bright caustic lines and blobs; its square shape survives only roughly.
  2. Edges of the light smear downward in thin tails, following the flow direction of the sheet of water.
  3. The four frames differ in detail although the light is the same. The film changes from moment to moment, so the distortion pattern should animate.
  4. Everything outside the light stays black; the water is visible only where something bright is behind it.

#### S2 · Defocused raindrops on a window at night, Brastad
- URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bokeh_raindrops_on_a_window_1.jpg
- Image: https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/01/Bokeh_raindrops_on_a_window_1.jpg/1280px-Bokeh_raindrops_on_a_window_1.jpg
- Shows: Window drops lit by street lights, fully out of focus. (cache g02)
- Observations:
  1. Each lit drop becomes a round disc of nearly the same size, about 3 to 5 percent of frame width. Disc size is set by the lens, not the drop.
  2. Many discs show an onion ring or edge-brighter texture rather than a flat fill.
  3. Colors split between warm sodium-like amber and neutral white. The background between discs is near black.
  4. Overlapping discs add brightness, so dense clusters near a light source form a bright mass with distinct disc edges at its border.

#### S3 · Raindrops on a window, Brastad, in focus
- URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Raindrops_on_a_window_in_Brastad_3.jpg
- Image: https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5a/Raindrops_on_a_window_in_Brastad_3.jpg/1280px-Raindrops_on_a_window_in_Brastad_3.jpg
- Shows: Sharp drops on a window pane against a dark left half and grey right half. (cache g03)
- Observations:
  1. Against the dark side, drops read as bright specular dots and small bright arcs. Against the grey side they read as darker shapes with a light point.
  2. Many drops are teardrops, flat or rounded on top and narrowing downward, the static shape before sliding.
  3. Drops are much denser on the dark left part; size is mostly 1 to 3 mm with a few larger.
  4. Each drop carries a small bright highlight plus a dark refraction body, the same two part read as beads on paint.

#### S4 · Rainy lights through a fogged window
- URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rainy_Lights.jpg
- Image: https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/60/Rainy_Lights.jpg/1280px-Rainy_Lights.jpg
- Shows: City lights through glass with condensation and drops, all soft. (cache g04)
- Observations:
  1. A condensation layer lowers contrast over the whole frame and lifts the blacks to a warm brown; nothing behind is sharp.
  2. Lights become large soft discs in amber, orange and violet; inside some discs the shapes of drops show as darker spots.
  3. Drops are concentrated in a vertical band on the right where water ran, leaving clearer glass beside it.
  4. Where drops have run through the fog, they leave clear vertical lanes, and those lanes show the lights more sharply.

#### S5 · Car window at night with city lights
- URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A_rainy_night.jpg
- Image: https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c4/A_rainy_night.jpg/1280px-A_rainy_night.jpg
- Shows: Wet car side window, amber and teal lights behind. (cache g05)
- Observations:
  1. Drops are dark bodies with thin bright edges against the bright amber light, and almost invisible against black areas.
  2. Running water draws vertical, slightly wavy streaks through the light, each a few drop widths long.
  3. A thin film smears the lights into soft glows with lumpy edges.
  4. The strongest light, top center, blooms into a broad warm mass; green and red sources keep their hue but lose shape.

#### S6 · Through the windshield on a rainy night, Porto
- URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dia_de_chuva_atrav%C3%A9s_do_para-brisas_(4391465624).jpg
- Image: https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c7/Dia_de_chuva_atrav%C3%A9s_do_para-brisas_%284391465624%29.jpg/1280px-Dia_de_chuva_atrav%C3%A9s_do_para-brisas_%284391465624%29.jpg

  1. With focus on the bridge, the drops on the glass become large soft discs, each lit by the warm lights behind.
  2. Discs carry internal streaks and texture from the drop shapes rather than being clean rounds.
  3. The bridge stays sharp through gaps between drops, so the scene behind reads as a sharp image under a soft spotted veil.

#### S7 · Hong Kong rainstorm through a window
- URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hong_Kong_Rainstorm_-_Flickr_-_tallkev.jpg
- Image: https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/53/Hong_Kong_Rainstorm_-_Flickr_-_tallkev.jpg/1280px-Hong_Kong_Rainstorm_-_Flickr_-_tallkev.jpg
- Shows: A city street canyon at dusk through rain streaked glass, traffic and neon below. (cache g07)
- Observations:
  1. Rivulets are thin, meandering, near-vertical lines spaced roughly 2 to 4 percent of frame width apart, catching the sky so they read light on dark buildings.
  2. Each rivulet wiggles with a short period, roughly every few rivulet widths, rather than running straight.
  3. The scene behind (towers, red sign, tail lights) is softened but readable; rivulets distort only a narrow strip each.
  4. The grade is cold dusk blue with small warm accents at street level; the glass water takes the color of what is behind.

#### S11 · Raindrops on taxi glass
- URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Raindrops_on_taxi_glass.jpg
- Image: https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/73/Raindrops_on_taxi_glass.jpg/1280px-Raindrops_on_taxi_glass.jpg
- Shows: Heavy water on a windshield, a yellow taxi ahead seen through it. (cache g12)
- Observations:
  1. Thick water distorts the taxi into wobbly shapes; straight edges of the car become wavy lines.
  2. Distortion is strongest where the film is thick and lowest in clear gaps between drops.
  3. Drops show dark edges against the bright sky band at top.
  4. Refraction shifts appear as local offsets of a few percent of frame width, not as uniform blur.

#### S12 · Water droplets on a car sunroof, Mansfield
- URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Water_droplets_on_a_car_sunroof,_Mansfield,_Massachusetts,_US_(PPL1-Corrected)_julesvernex2.jpg
- Image: https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/54/Water_droplets_on_a_car_sunroof%2C_Mansfield%2C_Massachusetts%2C_US_%28PPL1-Corrected%29_julesvernex2.jpg/1280px-Water_droplets_on_a_car_sunroof%2C_Mansfield%2C_Massachusetts%2C_US_%28PPL1-Corrected%29_julesvernex2.jpg

- Observations:
  1. On horizontal glass drops are large and flat, about 10 to 25 mm, irregular in outline.
  2. Each large drop has a dark refraction edge on one side, the side away from the sky, and a sky-bright center.
  3. Between large drops sits a field of micro drops under 1 mm, giving the glass a frosted grey.
  4. Flat drops are much wider and flatter than paint beads, consistent with glass wetting at a low contact angle.

### Real-world sizes
- Conventional windshield: two 2.1 mm soda-lime glass plies with a 0.76 mm PVB interlayer; lighter builds use 2.1 mm outer and 1.6 mm inner. Typical windshield area about 1.4 m². (source: https://www.corning.com/microsites/ags/downloads/white-papers/novel-lightweight-laminate-concept-for-automotive-windshields.pdf)
- Contact angle of water on soda-lime glass: below 15°. (source: https://technical.gelest.com/brochures/hydrophobicity-hydrophilicity-and-silane-surface-modification/water-hydrophobicity-and-hydrophilicity/)
- Typical raindrop diameter: 0.5 to 5 mm. Drops slide down a vertical pane when larger than about 2.7 mm; smaller drops may stick until another drop adds mass. (source: https://hal.science/hal-02343291v1/file/SlidingDropsToledano.pdf)
- Raindrops are near spherical below 1 mm, flattened above, and cannot exceed about 10 mm. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raindrop_size_distribution)
- Sliding drop shape stages: rounded front, then a cornered rear above a first critical speed, then a rivulet from the corner that breaks into droplets above a second critical speed. (source: https://people.maths.bris.ac.uk/~majge/PhysFluids_19_042104.pdf)

### Build notes
- Two layers: static drops (0.5 to 3 mm, round or teardrop, density 1 drop per 2 to 4 drop areas) and moving rivulets (width 1 to 2 mm, spacing 2 to 4 percent of view width on a side window).
- Rivulet path: mostly downward on a parked car, with lateral wander of 1 to 3 widths every 3 to 6 widths of travel. On a moving car tilt paths up to about 30° to 45° from vertical along the air flow (S8).
- Start sliding only for drops above 2.7 mm equivalent; give sliding drops a rounded front and pointed rear, and leave a string of 0.2 to 0.5 mm drops in the wake.
- Later drops should follow earlier tracks most of the time; new tracks only occasionally.
- Refraction: offset the background inside each drop by 2 to 6 percent of drop radius times drop curvature, and invert it inside drops over 2 mm. Add a dark rim 10 to 20 percent of radius on the side away from the brightest light.
- Water film: low frequency normal noise, amplitude about 1 to 2 percent of view width in background offset, animated at a few cycles per second in heavy rain. Smears lights downward.
- Fog or condensation: optional uniform veil raising blacks 3 to 6 percent with clear lanes where rivulets passed.
- Defocus: with focus past the glass, render lit drops as discs of fixed size per lens, onion texture on the disc, near black between. With anamorphic squeeze apply the same vertical oval as the rest of the frame.
- Drops are only visible where something bright is behind or reflected; keep drops in black areas close to invisible.
