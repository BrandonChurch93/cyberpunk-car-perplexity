## Wet metal, wet rubber, wet painted steel

What to learn: How each changes when wet

### Sources

#### S1 · Wet tire, macro
- URL: https://www.flickr.com/photos/80378665@N00/3429406230
- Image: https://live.staticflickr.com/3361/3429406230_0dc103dda7_b.jpg
- Shows: Close view of tire tread blocks and sipes covered in water drops.
- Observations:
  1. Water sits on the rubber as round beads, about 1 to 6 mm across, plus fine droplets of 0.2 to 0.5 mm.
  2. Each bead has a bright point highlight, a dark rim, and a bright caustic on the side away from the light.
  3. Rubber between the beads stays fairly matte, dark brown-black, with a soft sheen only on rib tops.
  4. Rib top edges catch thin bright line highlights.
  5. Beads sit both on the ribs and down in the grooves.

### Real-world sizes
- Water contact angle on galvanized steel: about 62 degrees (source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12251997/table/polymers-17-01796-t008/)
- Water contact angle on stainless steel: about 70.8 degrees (source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3769772/)
- Water contact angle on natural rubber vulcanizate: about 103.6 degrees (source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11644439/); about 107 degrees in another study (source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13665833/)
- Hot-dip galvanized coating: minimum average about 85 µm, up to about 140 µm, often with a spangle finish (source: https://steelconstruction.info/topics/corrosion-protection/metallic-coatings)
- Reactive steels galvanize to a matte gray, mottled finish (source: https://galvanizeit.org/uploads/publications/Galvanized_Steel_Inspection_Guide.pdf)
- Tyre tread depth new about 8 mm; legal minimum 1.6 mm (source: https://www.halfords.com/tyres/advice/tread-depth-and-safety.html)
- Capillary length of water about 2.71 mm at 20 °C, which caps how tall a large drop can stand before it flattens (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capillary_length)
- Raindrops 0.5 to 4 mm typical; under about 2 mm near spherical (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raindrop)

### Build notes
- Rubber dry: albedo about 0.04 to 0.06 linear (25 to 30 percent sRGB value, S3), roughness 0.75 to 0.9. Wet with film: albedo about 0.02 to 0.03, roughness 0.2 to 0.3 on exposed faces (knob tops, rib tops) so they get the black lacquer look (S2). Grooves stay rougher. Beads 1 to 6 mm on flat rubber (S1), from a contact angle above 100 degrees.
- Galvanized steel: metallic, base about 0.6 to 0.7 gray, roughness 0.4 to 0.55, plus a spangle or mottle mask at 5 to 30 mm scale. Wet: drops are flat (contact angle about 62 degrees), 1 to 6 mm, drawn darker than the zinc with a light rim (S2, S10). Do not drop the base roughness much; the zinc stays frosted.
- Stainless and brushed steel: wet shows as a sheet with holes (S13) or flattened drops up to about 15 mm, flat topped above about 5 mm (S14). Keep the anisotropic grain visible through the water.
- Gloss painted steel: dry roughness 0.25 to 0.4. Wet: dense beads of 0.5 to 4 mm on horizontal faces (S7, S11), teardrops about 2:1 on vertical faces (S4, S6), and a row of pendant drops on bottom edges at 1 to 2 drop widths spacing (S5, S6, S7). Base color under drops about 10 to 20 percent darker and more saturated (S4).
- Weathered or chipped paint: water forms flat pools with meniscus lines, not beads (S8). Use the chip mask to switch bead mode to pool mode.
- Rust: matte (roughness about 0.85 to 0.95) and darker when wet, with no beading (S4). Darken albedo about 30 percent wet and leave roughness high.
- Chrome: keep roughness near 0.05; drops show as dark-ringed lenses that break the mirror into speckle where dense (S5, S11).
- Cast iron: wet value about 20 percent (S12), sheen on flat tops, raised edges catching the sky.
- Every drop: bright point highlight, dark rim, bright caustic on the side away from the light (S1, S9, S14). At distance, replace drops with a speckle mask in the normal and roughness maps.
- Wetness kinds by material: beads on rubber and gloss paint, flat drops on galvanized steel, sheet on stainless and porous concrete, none on rust (it darkens only).
