# Key frame prompts

Key frames are generated images that set the target for mood, palette and composition. They are never used as a texture, a backdrop or a frame of the film. Where a key frame and a real photograph on the board disagree about physical behavior, the photograph wins.

- Generated 2026-11-19. All frames are 16:9. Files here are JPEG copies at the generated size.
- Three image tools were used. They are listed as image tool 1, 2 and 3. Which tool made which frame is in the tables below.
- The chosen direction is C. K1 scores are in `kit/PROGRESS.md`.

## Directions for the hero frame

Shared opening of all three prompts:

> A photograph, a still frame from a high-end car film shot on a full-frame cinema camera with an anamorphic lens. Subject: one Lamborghini Revuelto supercar with gloss black pearl paint, completely debadged, with no logos, no crests, no wordmarks and no number plate, parked alone and slightly askew across the painted bay lines on the open top deck of a multi-storey car park, at night, in steady heavy rain, surrounded on all sides by much taller towers of a very dense city covered in neon and LED signs. No people anywhere. The car's lamps are in the middle of switching on: the thin Y-shaped daytime running lights glow brightly, the main headlamps are just starting to light, and their beams are visible as cones where they cross the rain. The deck is worn sealed concrete, soaked, with shallow puddles in the low spots that carry rain rings and mirror the car and the signs; scuffed white bay lines, a drain grate, an expansion joint. Rain is visible as short bright streaks only where it is backlit by signs or crosses the headlamp beams, and almost invisible against dark areas. Thick haze separates the city into depth layers and gives every light a soft glow. A low cloud base is lit from below by the city. Water beads on the paint and runs off the lower edges. Signs show only simple generic words in Latin capitals such as PARKING, HOTEL, BAR, EXIT, OPEN, 24H; no real brand names. Natural film grain, soft highlight roll-off, shadows that keep detail, oval out-of-focus highlights, physically believable reflections.

## What the K1 jurors said to carry into the build

- Sign text that is garbled or repeated reads as generated. The build draws every sign from `signage` in the config, each word once per sight line.
- Out-of-focus highlights that are evenly sized and evenly spaced look placed. Bokeh in the build comes from real light sources at their real distances.
- A black car against a dark city recedes at phone size. The rim light and the reflections have to carry the silhouette in the poster frame.
- Oversaturated neon reads as a game render. Sign color stays inside the grade in `kit/04-LOOK.md`.
